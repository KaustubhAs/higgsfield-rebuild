# Original concept artwork

The vector illustrations in `components/ConceptArt.js` were authored for this project. They depict fictional product packaging, abstract sculpture and architectural forms. No screenshot imagery, third-party photographs, remote assets or external fonts are used.

These illustrations are available under CC0-1.0. They are concept artwork, not sample inference results. The interface labels them accordingly.

The procedural sample SVG illustrations and video storyboards in `lib/generation-provider.js` are also original project artwork available under CC0-1.0. They are explicitly labeled samples, never real AI inference. Video storyboards are static SVG files, not playable videos.

User-selected reference images are resized into thumbnails and stored locally with drafts, saved assets, and recipes. They are never uploaded or analyzed. They remain subject to the user's rights to the source image; the project's CC0 dedication does not apply to user-supplied references.
